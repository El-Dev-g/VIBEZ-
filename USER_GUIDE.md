# VIBEZ End User Guide

Welcome to **VIBEZ**, your secure, high-performance messaging and community platform. This guide will help you navigate the app and make the most of its features.

---

## 1. Getting Started: Onboarding
When you first open VIBEZ, you'll need to set up your digital identity.

*   **Phone Identity**: Enter your mobile number. The app will validate your number based on your country code. This number is your primary ID on the VIBEZ network.
*   **Profile Setup**: Choose a display name, an "About" status, and upload a profile photo. You can change these later in **Settings > Profile**.

## 2. Core Messaging
Messaging is at the heart of VIBEZ.

*   **Start a Chat**: Tap the **FAB (Floating Action Button)** on the Chats screen or use the **Select Contact** screen to find friends.
*   **Message Types**: You can send text, images, videos, and documents.
*   **Media Gallery**: View all photos, videos, and links shared in a specific chat by tapping the contact's profile and selecting **Media, links, and docs**.
*   **Starred Messages**: Long-press a message to "Star" it for quick retrieval later in the chat menu.

## 3. QR Discovery
Connect instantly without typing phone numbers.

*   **My QR Code**: Found in **Settings** or your **User Profile**. Show this to a friend to let them scan you.
*   **Scan QR**: Tap the QR icon in **Select Contact** or **Settings** to open the scanner. Point your camera at another user's QR code to add them instantly.

## 4. Status Updates (Stories)
Share your moments for 24 hours.

*   **Create Status**: Tap the camera or edit icon on the **Status** tab to share a photo, video, or text update.
*   **View Status**: See updates from your contacts at the top of the Status screen.

## 5. Communities & Groups
Organize your world.

*   **Groups**: Traditional group chats for family and friends.
*   **Communities**: Large-scale organizations that can house multiple sub-groups. Perfect for workplaces, schools, or hobby groups.

## 6. Calls
High-quality voice and video calling.

*   **Start a Call**: Open a chat and tap the **Phone** or **Video** icon at the top.
*   **Call History**: All your incoming, outgoing, and missed calls are tracked in the **Calls** tab.

## 7. Backend & Cloud Sync
VIBEZ is designed to be "Always Synced."

*   **Manual Sync**: If you feel your messages are out of date, go to **Settings > Backend & Cloud Sync** and tap **Match & Sync Everything Now**.
*   **Real-time Stream**: VIBEZ uses WebSocket technology to ensure your messages arrive instantly.

---

## Tips for a Better Experience
*   **Dark Mode**: VIBEZ supports a beautiful dark theme that follows your system settings.
*   **Privacy**: Control who sees your profile photo and status in **Settings > Privacy**.
*   **Help & Support**: Encountered a bug? Check your connection in the Backend Sync screen or contact your administrator.
